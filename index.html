    Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street

    Free and easy for the rest of the day. The street is free to walk; you only pay for food and shopping.

    • Taotaoju 陶陶居 Traditional Cantonese dim sum, including shrimp dumplings and roast goose
    • Dongji Xintiandi Plaza Large mall with fashion, shoes and local snacks
    • Lianxianglou 莲香楼 Cantonese dim sum, lotus-seed-paste mooncakes and soy sauce chicken
    • Liwan Plaza Six floors, with a jewellery wholesale market plus clothing and food stores
    • Lingnan arcades Qilou arcade buildings that mix European and Chinese styles, with Tanglong doors
